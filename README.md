# 🌙 PoemTale-Diffusion  
### *Minimising Information Loss in Poem-to-Image Generation with Multi-Stage Prompt Refinement*

Welcome to the official repository for **PoemTale-Diffusion**, introduced in our paper:  
**“PoemTale Diffusion: Minimising Information Loss in Poem to Image Generation with Multi-Stage Prompt Refinement.”**

This project explores a key challenge in AI-generated imagery:  
**How can diffusion models faithfully interpret the layered, symbolic, and abstract nature of poetic language?**

---

## ✨ Overview

While modern text-to-image diffusion models excel at generating realistic visuals, they often struggle with **poems**—a form of language rich in metaphor, abstraction, and dual meanings. Conventional models tend to lose essential information, generating images that miss the poem’s intended depth.

**PoemTale-Diffusion** presents a **training-free** solution designed specifically for poetry-to-image generation.

---


---

### 🎨 Consistent Self-Attention for Stable Diffusion  
We adapt state-of-the-art diffusion models by modifying their **self-attention mechanisms** to ensure:

- consistent generation across multiple images  
- stable feature representation  
- collective visual storytelling aligned with the poem  

Multiple consistent images help capture the poem’s layered meaning.

---



### 📝 Expert + Quantitative Evaluation  
A panel of poetry experts evaluated our outputs based on:

- semantic faithfulness  
- emotional tone  
- visual representation of abstract themes  

Quantitative metrics further confirm the improvements achieved by PoemTale-Diffusion.

---

## 🎯 Why PoemTale-Diffusion?

Poetry is not straightforward text.  
It is symbolic, layered, emotional, and intentionally ambiguous.

**PoemTale-Diffusion bridges the gap between poetic expression and visual generative models**, enabling AI systems to:

- reason about abstract language  
- preserve metaphorical meaning  
- generate cohesive artistic visuals  

This work pushes the boundaries of computational creativity and poetic understanding.
